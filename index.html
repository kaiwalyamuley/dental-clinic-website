Questions patients ask

Will the treatment hurt?

We numb the area before any procedure that could be uncomfortable, and we pause whenever you raise your hand. Most patients are surprised how easy it feels.

How much will it cost?

You get a written quote after your first consultation, before any treatment starts. If the plan changes, we tell you first.

Do I need an appointment?

Appointments are best, because they keep waiting times short. For severe pain, call us and we'll fit you in as soon as we can.

At what age should my child first visit?

Around the first birthday, or when the first tooth appears. Early visits prevent most childhood cavities.
